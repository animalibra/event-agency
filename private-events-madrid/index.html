PRIVATE EVENTS
with a point of view.

A birthday, a reunion, a proposal or a reason that has no name yet. We create private events in Madrid around the person, not a standard occasion.

What makes it yours

THE OCCASION IS ONLY THE BEGINNING.

Most event plans begin with a venue, a guest count and a format. We begin with the individual whose story gives those decisions meaning. A memory can suggest the menu. An obsession can shape the invitation. A private joke can become the moment everyone remembers.

From an intimate dinner to a lively party, we develop an original creative direction and plan its realization. The level of production follows the idea and your practical needs. There are no fixed packages.

Our role can include creative direction, location research, collaborators, guest experience, production planning and on-site coordination, agreed for each project.
