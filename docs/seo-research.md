# English search intent notes — 12 October 2026

This is a qualitative review of live English-language search results and the pages behind them, **not** keyword-volume or ranking research. No traffic, difficulty or conversion numbers are claimed.

## Observed search landscape

- Searches around “bespoke private event planner Madrid Spain” surface a mix of [experience-led providers](https://bea-events.com/), [corporate event companies](https://dcompany.com/) and [luxury destination management](https://www.andaluxevents.com/). Their offers span different audiences. ONLY YOU WOULD should keep “private”, “personal” and Madrid visible so the right visitor understands the scope quickly.
- “Destination celebrations Spain” overlaps strongly with [destination wedding and event planning](https://www.anitaspainevents.com/) and [luxury event design](https://antieluxuryevents.com/en/). Our distinct page centres on personal gatherings and international guests, without implying that weddings are the only or primary offer.
- “Bespoke experiences Spain” includes [bespoke city activities](https://maisonprive-experiences.com/bespoke.html) and [private travel design](https://tailormadesp.com/). Our experience page explains concept and production work and avoids claiming regulated travel services we have not established.

These observations validate **different search intents**, not the commercial demand or ranking potential of any keyword.

## Implementation and follow-up

The static HTML pages expose visible H1s, useful body copy and crawlable links without JavaScript. Titles/descriptions/canonicals are unique; sitemap and robots files list production routes only. Concept studies are not schema-marked as real projects. No review, rating, address or award schema is present.

After release, verify indexing and actual queries in Google Search Console, review inquiries by page, and revise titles and content based on evidence. Do not create automatic translations or duplicate location pages merely to target more keywords.
