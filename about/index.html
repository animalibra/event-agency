The person behind the idea

NOT A THEME.
A portrait.

ONLY YOU WOULD is a founder-led creative studio in Madrid. We turn the details that make a person themselves into an original celebration or experience.

Why this studio exists

PEOPLE ARE MORE INTERESTING THAN FORMATS.

At the centre of every meaningful celebration is a person. Their habits, the stories their friends repeat, a place they miss, a very specific sense of humour. These details deserve more attention than a standard event theme.

ONLY YOU WOULD creates original concepts for private events and tailor-made experiences in Madrid and, where feasible, across Spain. The studio's aim is to combine imagination with the planning needed to make a personal idea real.

Our three visual worlds show different emotional directions, from electric to intimate to exploratory. They are creative examples, not fixed service packages.
